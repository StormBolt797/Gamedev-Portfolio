Romeo Romeo

3-Level Stealth Prison Escape Platformer
GDAI Game Connect Challenge: Secured 4th place out of 22 participating IITs for the problem statement by Game Connect by GDAI in InterIIT Techmeet 14.0.

Romeo Romeo is a high-difficulty 2D platformer set across 3 carefully designed levels. Players must break out of prison confinement, avoiding security searchlights, patrolling cops, and helicopter pursuits using precision jumps and stealth timing.

• 3 Handcrafted Levels: Escalating difficulty from holding cell blocks up to the high-security rooftop escape.
• Stealth & Vision Mechanics: Guard line-of-sight detection cones, hiding spots, and alert states.
• Movement & Physics: Wall jumps, momentum boosts, and responsive platforming controls.
Unity 2D C# Stealth AI WebGL Level Design

BoltRace

3D Arcade Racing with Gear Shift System & In-Game Shop

BoltRace is a 3D arcade racing game developed for Android with a complete campaign progression and defined ending. It features tactile gear shifting, racing alongside 2 other cars, and an in-game garage shop.

• In-Game Garage Shop: Unlock and drive 3 different cars with distinct handling profiles.
• 6 Unique Racing Arenas: Varied track layouts and environments with increasing challenge.
• Multi-Car Competition: Race on the track against 2 other cars.
• Tactile Gear System: Timed manual gear shifts to manage acceleration and top speed.
• Two Game Modes: Race Against Time Mode (beat the timer) and Race to Finish First Mode (race to finish first against rival cars).
Unity 3D C# Android SDK Track Racing Logic Mobile UI & Shop System

🕹️ Rank 2,605 / 3,501 • The Very Serious Juniper Dev Game Jam

Ballerina Cappuccina

3D Physics-Driven 1v1 Arena Brawler

A fast-paced local 1v1 arena combat game inspired by Beyblade battles. Built for The Very Serious Juniper Dev Game Jam (ranking 2,605 out of 3,501 entries), it combines rotational physics, centrifugal collision forces, dynamic camera toggles, and an in-match armor economy.

• Physics-Driven Centrifugal Combat: Collision impacts calculated directly from rotational angular momentum and velocity.
• 3-Way Armor Customization: In-game shop to buy armor specialized in Defense (sturdiness), Attack (impact force), or Speed (rapid movement).
• Local Split-Screen with 3 Dynamic Cameras: Real-time toggleable tactical camera views for 2-player local matches.
• Center Arena Power-Ups: Attack multipliers, heavy defense shields, and RPM/spin recovery charges.
Unity 3D C# Rigidbody Physics Split-Screen System Shop & Money Logic

SpaceBuster

Arcade Space Shooter • 10 Levels with Boss Battle
Developer Note: SpaceBuster was my first published game on the Google Play Store, focused on touch steering, space combat, and a 10-level progression with a final boss fight.

A classic arcade space shooter where players navigate through asteroid fields, shoot incoming enemy craft, collect power-up drops, and progress through 10 structured levels culminating in a final boss encounter.

• 10 Levels & Final Boss: Level-by-level progression leading to a dedicated boss encounter on level 10.
• Asteroid Dodging & Shooting: Responsive touch steering to weave through dense obstacles and blast enemies.
• Collectible Power-Ups: Collectible drops to increase firing rate and survivability.
• Visual FX: Clean 2D explosion particle effects and projectile feedback.
